For a hard hour

Things to reach for

Ride it out

A craving is a wave, not a straight line. Most peak and fall inside a quarter of an hour. Start the timer and just get to the end of it.

Run the four checks

Before you decide what a bad hour means, check whether it is really one of these four. It very often is.

Said at the end of most meetings

God grant me the serenity to accept the things I cannot change, courage to change the things I can, and wisdom to know the difference.

Any hour, any day

Need to talk to someone?

You don’t have to be in trouble to call. Wanting to talk is reason enough.

Working towards

Sobriety chips started in the 1940s, when a nun in Akron gave every patient leaving her ward a small medallion and asked one thing: if you’re going to drink, give it back to me first. A chip isn’t a prize. It’s something to hold on to on the hard days.
